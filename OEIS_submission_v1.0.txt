Name
Decimal expansion of -Integral_{x=1..infinity} log(abs(sin(Pi*x)))/x^2 dx.

Data

Offset

Keywords
nonn,cons

Formula
Equals -Integral_{x=1..infinity} log(abs(sin(Pi*x)))/x^2 dx, denoted by C_g.
Equals Pi^2/2 - log(2*Pi) - 1 - 2*Sum_{k>=1} (zeta(2*k)-1)/(2*k-1).
Equals Pi^2/2 - log(2*Pi) - 1 - Sum_{m>=2} log((m+1)/(m-1))/m.
a(n) = floor(10^(n+1)*C_g) - 10*floor(10^n*C_g).
Equals lim_{p prime, p->infinity} -log(abs(Product_{k=2..p-1} sin(Pi*p/k)))/p.
