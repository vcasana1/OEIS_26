Decimal enclosure for C_g
Recorded computation from the OEIS preparation session, November 11, 2026

Method: mpmath 1.4.1 interval arithmetic, 150 decimal digits of working
precision, N = 180 terms of the even-zeta series. The Bernoulli numbers
were generated as exact rational numbers. The one-sided analytic
truncation bound was 2 * 4^(-180).

Lower endpoint and upper endpoint, scaled by 10^100 and floored, gave
the same integer:
D = 7437948618394005442036419946345806610252643319729172229603543808135540028677299838717570371013522332

Width of the working enclosure before scaling, as recorded: about
8.51591968e-109.

Exact decimal enclosure implied by the equal floors:
D/10^100 <= C_g < (D+1)/10^100.

The digits in the article and in the OEIS draft were transcribed from D.
